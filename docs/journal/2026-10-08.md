# 2026-10-08 — Catch-up: five weeks since 2026-09-06

The journal stopped at 2026-09-06 while the work kept going. This entry
records what shipped since, by theme, with the PR to read for detail. It
is a summary of merged PR descriptions and the module docs, not a fresh
verification of each: where a PR or doc recorded a production check, that
is said; otherwise assume only CI.

## Phase 8 CP1–CP4, and the close-out

- **CP1 and CP2 (Reports v1, "Recording Completeness")** — built and
  verified in production 2026-09-13/14 (`docs/modules/phase-8.md` §15–§16).
- **CP3 (timetable builder)** — bell schedule and the builder, verified in
  production with an edge-gate fix (§17; recorded in #304).
- **CP4 (#304)** — timetable lifecycle: fork and copy with preview,
  publish/withdraw as per-class snapshots, teacher and family read surfaces,
  clash rules that count active classes only and refuse only a clash a
  change adds (D43–D45).
- **Close-out (#305)** — Phase 8 closed after CP4; the results work split
  into Phase 8c (D46).

## The school day (`docs/modules/the-school-day.md`)

- **Absence alerts and homework (#346)** — a parent is told when their child
  is marked absent (once per guardian per day, held 15 minutes so a mistyped
  register can be corrected, no child's name on the lock screen); homework,
  with the one reply path a family has.
- **Behaviour records (#351)** — staff-only by construction: no family
  method, controller, DTO or permission exists, and no AI touches them.
- **Announcements (#338, #341)** — owner/admin to everyone, parents, staff or
  one class; never edited, only withdrawn; delivered through the
  notification rail (#336), which covers staff devices, quiet hours and
  once-per-person.
